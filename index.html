About

Hello! I am an incoming Research Scientist at the Superintelligence Lab, LG AI Research. Prior to that, I received my M.S. from KAIST AI, where I was advised by Prof. Kijung Shin at the Data Mining Lab, and my B.S. in Applied Statistics and Computer Science from Yonsei University.

My research interests lie in Information Retrieval, especially in Multimodal Retrieval, Structured Retrieval and Agentic Search. I am also interested in Multimodal Learning, particularly Vision-Language Models (VLMs) and Omni-modal Models.
